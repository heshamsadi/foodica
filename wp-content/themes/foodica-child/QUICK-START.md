# 🎯 QUICK START: CREATE YOUR FIRST RECIPE

**Time Needed:** 15 minutes  
**No Coding Required** ✅

---

## 📝 STEP-BY-STEP

### 1. Create New Post
```
Dashboard → Posts → Add New
```

### 2. Add Title & Image
- **Title:** "Easy Creamy Garlic Parmesan Pasta"
- **Featured Image:** Click "Set featured image" (1200×600px)

### 3. Scroll Down to "📋 Recipe Details" Box

### 4. Fill In the Form

**📝 Introduction** (2-3 paragraphs):
```
This creamy garlic parmesan pasta is pure comfort food. Rich sauce, 
bold garlic, nutty parmesan—ready in 20 minutes!
```

**⏱️ Timing:**
- Prep: 5 minutes
- Cook: 15 minutes
- Total: 20 minutes

**🍽️ Nutrition:**
- Servings: 4 servings
- Calories: 520 kcal

**🥕 Ingredients** (one per line):
```
• 1 pound fettuccine
• 4 tablespoons butter
• 4 cloves garlic, minced
• 2 cups heavy cream
• 1 1/2 cups Parmesan cheese
• Salt and pepper to taste
```

**👨‍🍳 Directions** (numbered steps):
```
1. Boil pasta according to package directions.
2. Melt butter, sauté garlic for 1-2 minutes.
3. Add cream, simmer 3-4 minutes.
4. Whisk in Parmesan cheese until smooth.
5. Toss pasta with sauce and serve hot.
```

**💡 Tips** (optional):
```
• Use fresh Parmesan for best results
• Don't let sauce boil after adding cheese
• Add grilled chicken for extra protein
```

### 5. Add Categories & Tags
- **Categories:** Pasta, Italian, Quick Dinners
- **Tags:** pasta, italian, creamy, garlic, quick

### 6. Click "Publish" ✅

## 💡 PRO TIPS

1. **Write like you're talking to a friend** - Casual, helpful tone

3. **Add visual cues** - "Until golden brown" or "fluffy"
4. **Test your recipe** - Make sure it actually works!
5. **Use high-quality photos** - Natural lighting is best
